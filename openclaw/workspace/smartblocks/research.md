## Developer Pain Points (2024–2025)
1. **Capital is tight** — traditional fundraising is relationship-dependent and slow
2. **Interest rates hurt deal economics** — developers need alternative capital stacks
3. **Blockchain skepticism** — many developers don't understand tokenization or trust it
4. **Compliance fear** — SEC, securities law, liability concerns
5. **Finding the right investors** — limited to personal networks and brokers
6. **High platform fees** on existing crowdfunding tools

### What Developers Actually Want
- Fast access to a larger investor pool
- Simple onboarding (not 6-month legal processes)
- Credibility signals (who else has listed? what returns?)
- Low fees / better economics than traditional syndication
- Clear legal/compliance framework
