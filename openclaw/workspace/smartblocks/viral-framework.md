## What Actually Makes Content Go Viral (2025 Data)

### The 4 Core Triggers (every viral post hits at least one)
1. **Curiosity** — opens a loop the reader must close
2. **Emotion** — makes them feel something (surprise, validation, FOMO, pride)
3. **Relevance** — speaks directly to their specific situation
4. **Surprise** — contradicts expectations or conventional wisdom

---

## The Patterns (from real data)

### 1. Contradictions & Contrast (~30% of top performers)
Your brain can't scroll past unresolved tension.
> "I stopped chasing investors. They started finding me."
> "Tokenization sounds complicated. It's actually the simplest deal structure you'll use."
> "We built a marketplace with no marketing budget. Here's what happened."

### 2. The Specificity Effect
Generic loses. Hyper-specific wins. Speak to ONE person.
> ❌ "Real estate developers need more capital"
> ✅ "If you've ever lost a deal because your capital raise took 4 months, read this"

### 3. Timeframe Tension
Unexpected timeframes create dopamine + curiosity loops.
> "3 months ago we had 0 properties listed. Here's what changed."
> "I raised $2M for a real estate deal in 11 days. No broker. No phone bank."
> "In 2020 tokenized RE was $200M. Today it's $50B. You're early."

### 4. POV = Advice in Disguise
People's defenses drop when they think they're just relating to a scenario.
> "POV: You're a developer who just lost a deal because your capital took too long to close"
> "POV: You just found out a property you passed on raised $1.2M in 3 weeks on a tokenization platform"

### 5. Human Posts Beat AI Posts 3.7x
In 2025, 53% of LinkedIn posts are AI-generated. Human, personal, first-person posts get 3.7x more engagement.
**Implication:** Mark needs to write in his own voice. Founder story + personal moments >> corporate copy.

### 6. Contrarian Take
Challenge the dominant narrative in the space.
> "Everyone says tokenization is the future. Here's why most platforms will fail."
> "Stop syndication. You're leaving money on the table."
> "Your investors don't need to be accredited. Most developers don't know this."

### 7. The Confession / Vulnerability Hook
> "I have a confession..."
> "I was wrong about real estate crowdfunding for 3 years."
> "We almost didn't build this. Here's why I'm glad we did."

## LinkedIn-Specific Rules (2025)
- **First 3 lines = everything.** Only ~200 chars show before "See more" — that's your entire hook budget
- **No links in the post body** — LinkedIn suppresses external links. Put links in comments
- **Native video > everything** — outperforms all other formats
- **Line breaks matter** — short punchy lines, lots of white space, easy to skim
- **End with a question or CTA** to drive comments (comments = algorithmic rocket fuel)
- **3-5 hashtags max** at the end

## Twitter/X-Specific Rules
- Thread openers: bold claim first, "thread 🧵" second line
- Contrarian > informational
- Specific numbers and data outperform vague statements
- End threads with a CTA ("Follow me for more", "RT if this helped")
