## Neurons
> Rules:
    Neurons that "fire together, wire together" meaning they remain attached across space 
    Neurons inherently hold position:
        EVERY NEURON FALLS UNDER ONE OF THESE 
        1. Sensory neurons : Convert environmental input to electrical signals
        2. Motor neurons : transmit signals from CNS to muscles and glands
        3. Interneurons : connect neurons to neurons. The brain's highway system

        NEXT, from each, a neuron can either be:
        1. Multipolar : One axon tail, many dendrites
        2. Bipolar : two axon tails, many dendrites
        3. Unipolar : single projection?? so one dendrite one axon?
        4. Pseudounipolar : single axon splits into two directions, best handles touch / pain & pressure

        CAN BE REGULATED BY ANY OF THESE NEUROTRANSMITTERS:
        1. **Glutamatergic: excitatory & causes firing --> acts as a main accelerator
        2. **GABAergic : inhibitory & causes firing --> main brake
        3. Dopaminergic : does not cause firing (modulatory) --> signals whether something WAS good or bad. Emergent trait is "wanting"
        4. Serotonin : also modulatory. The "perfume" or background smell of the system. Sets mood 
        5. Acetylcholine : Attention and arousal. Is the "lockwarden" to what gets processed
        6. Norepinephrine : alertness / stress response. Signals threat or urgency, which prompts the increase of arousal, focuses attention, prepares body for action
        7. Endorphins : pain suppression and direct reward (akin to serotonin). Released under stress of pleasure, Modulate how much the system registers pain signals
