NAV Premium
—
market cap minus equity NAV
How much more MSTR's market cap is than its net asset value (BTC holdings minus debt). Positive = investors are paying a premium for the strategy itself, not just the Bitcoin. A large premium means the stock is expensive relative to its underlying assets.
mNAV = 1× at
—
BTC price for fair value
The Bitcoin price at which MSTR's market cap would equal its net BTC asset value — a 1× mNAV means zero premium, or "fair value." If BTC rose to this level, you'd be paying exactly for the Bitcoin held minus debt, with no extra premium for the strategy.
