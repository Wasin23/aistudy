Words are not the unit of reasoning—concepts are.
Each concept carries rules (constraints + affordances), not just meaning.
Reasoning is the interaction of those rules, not pattern matching of words.
Objects in the mind are structured systems, not labels.
Constraints define what must be true; affordances define what can happen.
Understanding a situation = activating relevant objects and applying their rules.
Ambiguity is resolved by selecting the most normal interaction under constraints.
Default behavior (intended use) dominates unless contradicted by new information.
Reasoning is selecting valid interactions, not generating text.
LLMs fail because they operate on token associations, not constraint-bound objects.
A real reasoning system must map language → structured objects → rule-based interaction.
